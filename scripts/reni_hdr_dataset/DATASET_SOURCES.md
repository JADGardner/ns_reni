# Source Provenance

RENI HDR was curated in 2022 from outdoor equirectangular HDR environment maps
made available under the CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication. The
source collections documented in the RENI++ paper and thesis are:

- Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/hdris/
- GiantCowFilms: https://giantcowfilms.com/category/hdris/
- iHDRI: https://www.ihdri.com/hdri-skies-outdoor/
- HDRMAPS: https://hdrmaps.com/
- Whitemagus 3D Models: http://www.whitemagus3dmodels.com/
- HDRI Skies: https://hdri-skies.com/
- Textures.com: https://www.textures.com/

Only assets represented as CC0 at the time of collection were selected. The
panoramas were checked manually and files containing personally identifiable
information, offensive content, or predominantly unnatural light sources were
removed.

The original source filenames were not retained when the curated files were
sequentially renamed. A reliable per-image source mapping therefore cannot be
reconstructed and is not claimed by this release. `CONTENTS.json` provides a
cryptographic manifest of the exact curated files instead.
